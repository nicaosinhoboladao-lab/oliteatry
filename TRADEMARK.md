# Trademark policy

The Apache-2.0 licence covers the **source code**. It does not grant rights to the OLiteatry name,
the jester mark or the visual identity of the official builds.

You may:

- fork, modify, redistribute and sell services around the code;
- state that your project "is based on OLiteatry";
- use the name in factual, descriptive references.

You may not:

- ship a modified build under the name "OLiteatry" or with the official mark in a way that implies
  it is the upstream release;
- use the mark in advertising for a derived product without permission.

Forks should rename the product and replace `build/icon.png` and the mark in
`src/renderer/index.html`. That's it — no paperwork.
